# Phone access

The current iPhone route is the free Home Screen web app. Follow [iPhone setup and notification tests](IPHONE-SETUP.md). Real notifications require adding Orby to the Home Screen and granting permission. Native Live Activities remain deferred.

Windows 10.44 supports a five-minute handoff for still-pending, accessible ChatGPT/Codex questions and permissions, exact signed answer choices, custom responses where the current form provides a writable field, and a narrowly matched Stop action. Other provider controls remain on the PC. All three providers' readable task contexts can appear as compact phone summaries.

The PC, relay and tunnel must stay online and awake. Neither Home Screen notifications nor a compiled native project makes the PC work while asleep. The withdrawn MCP connector is not used. Personal water tracking stays in the private Windows edition.

Android and native iPhone sources remain separate prototypes. They do not automatically gain the new Home Screen question-response UI or notification setup. Their physical background behavior and native signing/push delivery remain unverified.
