# Phone access

For the current Windows + iPhone setup, follow [Connect your iPhone for free](IPHONE-SETUP.md). Use the QR code in Orby 10.43, connect in Safari with a passkey, and compare the fingerprint on Windows. Keep the phone screen open. No background Live Activity or phone notification is included in this free route.

The PC and its temporary HTTPS helpers must remain online and awake. Task viewing covers ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini where desktop context is readable. Phone answering is opt-in and limited to one current, accessible ChatGPT/Codex Allow once / Deny permission card. Other permission controls and questions stay on the PC.

## Optional native clients

The user is testing iPhone first. Android is not needed for that workflow. The existing Android 11+ test APK uses the relay address and a five-minute invitation from Windows. Give the phone a name, connect, compare its fingerprint on both screens, then trust it on Windows. Screen lock is required for signed decisions. Its optional two-hour ongoing activity card is separate from Apple's Live Activities. Compilation and package-signature checks do not prove device/background behavior.

The native iOS 17.2+ app and ActivityKit extension compile on a cloud Mac, but remain unsigned and untested on a physical phone. Signing, TestFlight and APNs are deferred. The free Safari route does not install that native app or reuse its background capabilities.

The withdrawn MCP plugin and historical hook prototype are not part of this setup. You do not need access to Work chats on the phone. Personal water tracking stays in the private Windows edition.
